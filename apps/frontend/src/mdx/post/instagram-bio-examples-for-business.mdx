Your Instagram bio has a small space to do an important job: explain what your business does and give people a reason to take the next step. It does not need to tell your whole story. It needs to make the next action obvious.

## A simple formula

Try this structure:

**What you offer + who it is for + where you are + next step**

For example:

- Handmade ceramics for slow mornings · Seoul · See the new collection below
- Independent bakery making naturally leavened bread · Busan · Order this week’s menu
- Brand photographer for thoughtful small businesses · London · View recent work

Replace the details with your own voice, but keep the meaning easy to scan.

## Five bio examples by business type

### Local shop

“Seasonal flowers and small gifts for everyday celebrations. Pickup in Mapo. See today’s hours and directions below.”

Link in bio destinations: store hours, location, current collection, and contact.

### Consultant

“I help early teams make their product easier to understand and use. Remote sessions available. Book an introductory call.”

Link in bio destinations: booking, case studies, services, and email.

### Restaurant

“Neighborhood noodles, open late. Walk-ins welcome in Seongsu. Browse the menu and find tonight’s location below.”

Link in bio destinations: menu, map, reservations, and current announcement.

### Artist

“Painter and teacher exploring color, memory, and everyday places. New work and class dates below.”

Link in bio destinations: portfolio, shop, classes, and social profiles.

### Online store

“Small-batch skincare made with simple ingredients. Shop the current collection, read the routine guide, or ask a question below.”

Link in bio destinations: collection, best sellers, guide, and support.

## Make the link in bio page match the bio

The profile copy sets an expectation. The page should continue it. If your bio says “book a consultation,” put the booking link near the top. If it says “find us in Seongsu,” make the location easy to find.

Avoid sending people to a page that makes them repeat the search they already started on Instagram. A link in bio page should remove steps, not add them.

## A final check before publishing

Read your bio and tap the first link as if you have never heard of the business. Do you know what is being offered? Do you know where to go? Do you know what to do next? If the answer is yes, the page is doing its job.
